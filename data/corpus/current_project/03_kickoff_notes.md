## Confirmed decisions

- The baseline markets are Germany, France, and the United Kingdom.
- The total quantitative sample remains 1,500 completes.
- Recent churner means a customer who left ConnectTel within the previous six months.
- The research will cover prepaid and postpaid consumer customers; business accounts remain excluded.
- ConnectTel's Maya Chen is the final client approver for questionnaire and material scope decisions.
- Questionnaire approval is targeted for October 5, 2026.

## Discussion

ConnectTel asked that the questionnaire balance diagnostic detail with a target median completion time under twelve minutes. Network quality, value for money, support resolution, digital self-service, plan flexibility, and trust were confirmed as required domains.

## Open questions
