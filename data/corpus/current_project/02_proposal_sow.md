# ConnectTel Research Proposal and Statement of Work

## Agreed design

The supplier will conduct a mixed-method customer-experience and churn study in Germany, France, and the United Kingdom. Quantitative fieldwork will include 1,500 completed interviews, followed by fifteen qualitative interviews selected from respondents who consent to follow-up contact.

## Quantitative sample

The 1,500 completes will be divided equally across the three approved markets: 500 Germany, 500 France, and 500 United Kingdom. Within each market, the working target is 350 current customers and 150 recent churners. Recent churners are consumers who ended their primary ConnectTel mobile relationship during the previous six months.

## Qualitative sample

Fifteen sixty-minute remote interviews will be conducted: five in each approved market. Recruitment will balance current customers considering switching and recent churners. Interviews require separate consent and will not collect payment credentials or account passwords.

## Analysis

The supplier will provide descriptive results, market comparisons, churn-intent driver analysis, and a pragmatic segmentation. Any model described as predictive must be validated on a held-out sample or labelled exploratory. Small subgroups will be flagged rather than over-interpreted.

## Deliverables and acceptance

The final presentation is due October 20, 2026. Deliverables are accepted when they address the documented objectives, clearly label sample bases, distinguish current-customer and recent-churner results, and cite the relevant quantitative or qualitative evidence.

## Assumptions

- ConnectTel provides questionnaire feedback within two business days.
- Questionnaire approval is required before programming begins.
- Programming requires three working days after approval.
- Testing requires two working days after programming.
- Quantitative fieldwork requires ten calendar days after testing approval.
- Analysis requires five working days after the final clean dataset.
- Reporting requires five working days after analysis sign-off.
- Market additions or sample increases require written change approval covering price and timing.

## Exclusions

This statement of work does not include Spain, enterprise accounts, in-person interviews, passive mobile-device tracking, or linkage to identifiable billing records.
