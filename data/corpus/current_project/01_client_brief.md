# ConnectTel Customer Experience and Churn Study

## Background

ConnectTel is a fictional European telecommunications provider. Voluntary mobile-customer churn increased during the first half of 2026, particularly among customers with twelve to thirty-six months of tenure. Leadership needs evidence about the customer experiences and service interactions most associated with consideration of switching providers.

## Business objective

Identify the principal drivers of mobile-customer churn, define actionable at-risk customer segments, and recommend customer-experience improvements that ConnectTel can prioritize during 2027 planning.

## Research objectives

1. Measure overall satisfaction, likelihood to recommend, likelihood to renew, and stated likelihood to switch.
2. Assess perceptions of network reliability, price and value, customer support, digital self-service, plan flexibility, and brand trust.
3. Compare current customers with customers who left ConnectTel in the previous six months.
4. Identify attitudinal and behavioral segments associated with elevated churn risk.
5. Explain the experiences behind the quantitative patterns through follow-up interviews.

## Markets and audiences

The approved brief covers Germany, France, and the United Kingdom. The quantitative audience is adult consumer mobile-account decision-makers. Both prepaid and postpaid customers are eligible. The study should include current customers and recent churners. Business accounts and customers younger than eighteen are excluded.

## Methodology

The preferred design is mixed method: an online quantitative survey followed by fifteen remote qualitative interviews. The research partner may recommend refinements but must document assumptions that affect cost or timing.

## Deliverables

- Final research plan and sample design
- Approved multilingual questionnaire
- Fieldwork status updates twice per week
- Clean respondent-level dataset and codebook
- Topline findings presentation
- Segment profiles and churn-driver analysis
- Final executive presentation with prioritized recommendations
- Fifteen anonymized interview summaries

## Timing

Project kickoff is expected during the week of August 10, 2026. ConnectTel needs the final executive presentation by October 20, 2026 for its annual planning meeting.

## Constraints and governance

ConnectTel must approve the questionnaire, translations, and any material scope change before fieldwork begins. Personally identifying respondent data may not be placed in presentation materials. Recommendations must distinguish observed evidence from interpretation.

## Open questions

The brief does not identify an approved budget for adding markets after kickoff. The final definition of recent churner and the availability of CRM tenure data must be confirmed during kickoff.
