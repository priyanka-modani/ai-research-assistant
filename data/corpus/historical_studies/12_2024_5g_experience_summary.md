# 2024 5G Adoption and Network Experience Study

## Context

This fictional historical study covered the United Kingdom and France and focused on 5G adoption rather than customer churn. It is included as a related but imperfect historical source.

## Findings

Perceived indoor coverage and consistency mattered more to overall network satisfaction than peak download speed. Customers frequently used "5G" as a shorthand for the whole mobile experience, even when their complaint related to indoor coverage or handset compatibility.

## Reuse guidance

Network measures may be useful for the ConnectTel questionnaire, but the study did not test billing transparency, recent churners, or service-recovery experiences. It should not be used as evidence that network quality is the primary churn driver in the current project.
