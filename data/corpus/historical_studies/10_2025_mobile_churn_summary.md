# 2025 Mobile Customer Churn Study - Research Summary

## Context

This fictional historical study examined mobile-customer churn in Germany and France. It included 1,200 online interviews and twelve qualitative follow-ups. It is reference material only and does not establish facts or commitments for the 2026 ConnectTel project.

## Main findings

Network reliability remained important, but billing transparency was the strongest controllable experience associated with stated churn intent after accounting for overall price perception and tenure. Customers reporting unexpected charges or difficulty understanding their bill were substantially more likely to consider switching.

First-contact support resolution was also associated with retention. Qualitative interviews suggested that unresolved billing contacts were particularly damaging because they combined financial uncertainty with poor service recovery.

## Segment learning

The historical team described a group called "Uncertain Value Seekers." These customers did not always have the highest monthly price, but they struggled to predict charges and did not understand why their bill changed. Clear explanations and proactive charge notifications were recommended.

## Reuse guidance

The billing-transparency measures should be considered in future churn trackers when trend continuity matters. They should be adapted only after checking current objectives, terminology, market regulations, and questionnaire length. Historical results should not be presented as current ConnectTel findings.
