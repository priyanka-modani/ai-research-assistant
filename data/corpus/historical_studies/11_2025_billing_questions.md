# 2025 Mobile Churn Questionnaire - Billing Module

These fictional historical questions are provided for possible adaptation. They are not approved questions for the current ConnectTel project.

## Q12. Bill clarity

How easy or difficult is it to understand the charges shown on your mobile bill? 1 Very difficult; 2 Difficult; 3 Neither; 4 Easy; 5 Very easy.

## Q13. Charge predictability

Before receiving your bill, how confident are you that you know approximately how much you will be charged? 1 Not at all confident to 5 Completely confident.

## Q14. Unexpected charges

During the previous six months, have you noticed a charge on your mobile bill that you did not expect? Yes; No; Not sure.

## Q15. Explanation of changes

When your bill amount changes, how satisfied are you with the explanation provided? 1 Completely dissatisfied to 5 Completely satisfied; Bill has not changed.

## Usage note

If reused, question numbers must be reassigned and local terminology should be reviewed. The new study team should not claim trend comparability unless wording, population, and method are sufficiently consistent.
